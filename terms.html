2. WHAT DIP IS

Dip is a social app where users can be "traded" like stocks using D, a virtual, in-app currency. D has no cash value, cannot be purchased with real money, cannot be redeemed, exchanged, or cashed out for real currency or anything of value outside the app, and exists solely for entertainment and engagement within Dip. Prices, rankings, and returns shown in Dip are not real financial instruments and do not represent real investment gains or losses.

Profiles as tradable stocks. Creating an account does not list your stock. Your profile (your name, username, profile photo, bio, links, and posts) is visible to other signed-in users, but other users can buy and sell shares of you only after you choose to list your stock and confirm that choice in the app. You can use Dip, and trade other people's listed stocks, without listing your own. Once you list, other users can buy and sell shares of you using D, you earn a royalty in D on trades of your shares, and listing cannot be undone while your account exists; your stock is delisted only when you delete your account. Share prices reflect trading activity inside Dip only. They do not measure your real-world worth, and holding a share gives no one any ownership of, or rights over, you or your real-life identity. Your email address is never shown to other users. You can block any user, and you can delete your account at any time, which removes your profile from Dip.

What other users can see. Your public profile, D balance, stock holdings, share quantities, average purchase prices, and trading performance are visible to other signed-in users. Your trades may also appear in each stock's trade history.
