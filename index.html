Support

Dip is a social app where people are traded like stocks using D, a virtual in-app currency. D has no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, or redeemed for real money.

Contact & support

Questions, bug reports, or account help: getdip.app@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Report impersonation or objectionable content

You can report any post inside the app (… menu → Report) and block any user from their profile. To report an account that is impersonating you, email us with a link or username. We review reports and act on objectionable content within 24 hours.

Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy for what is removed.
