Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account information: your email (kept private and never shown to other users), name, username, profile photo, bio, and any links you add. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile details that provider shares with us.

Activity data: posts, likes, trades, holdings, watchlist, blocks, reports, and other actions you take in Dip.

Device and usage data: basic technical information (like device type and app version) used to keep the app running smoothly and to diagnose problems.

We do not collect payment card information, government ID numbers, or other sensitive financial identifiers — D is a virtual currency and doesn't involve real payment processing.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to operate Dip's core features — showing your profile, processing trades, calculating prices, displaying your feed, and enabling you to connect with other users. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand how the app is used and to improve it.

3. HOW WE STORE YOUR INFORMATION

Dip's data is stored using Firebase (a Google Cloud service). We apply reasonable technical safeguards to protect your information, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us run Dip (such as our cloud hosting provider), or when required by law. Other signed-in users can see your public profile, posts, D balance, stock holdings, share quantities, average purchase prices, and trading activity — since that's core to how Dip works.

5. YOUR CHOICES

You can edit or remove most of your profile information at any time from Edit Profile. You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account triggers an attempt to close your positions, removes your remaining D balance, delists your stock (if you listed one) as described in our Terms of Service, and removes your profile, posts, follows, and photos from Dip. Records of settled trades and settlement outcomes may be kept so that other users' balances and holdings stay accurate.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Dip is not directed at children under the minimum age required in your country to use the app, and we do not knowingly collect information from users who don't meet that requirement. If you believe a minor has created an account in violation of this, please contact us.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy as Dip evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" note above when we do.

8. CONTACT

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to getdip.app@gmail.com.
